# Persian Gulf Blockchain (PGB)

Persian Gulf Blockchain (PGB) is an independent Layer 1 blockchain built with Polkadot SDK (Substrate), focused on digital ownership, tokenization and real-world applications.

PGB aims to provide a shared infrastructure where developers, businesses and organizations can build blockchain applications across sports, culture and industry.

## Areas of Focus

- **Sports and culture:** fan tokens, community participation, digital collectibles and NFT-based ticketing.
- **Industry and business:** tokenization of real-world assets, product traceability and verifiable digital records.
- **Application development:** a foundation for blockchain services and applications tailored to organizational and user needs.

These areas represent the project's intended applications and development direction.

## Vision

Our vision is to connect blockchain technology with everyday economic and social activity, enabling transparent digital ownership and new ways for organizations and communities to exchange value.

Rooted in Iran and oriented toward international collaboration, PGB aims to grow through practical applications, developer participation and an expanding validator ecosystem.
